Chart of Accounts

Every account the business can post to. Contra accounts such as accumulated depreciation and owner's drawings keep the opposite normal balance from their type.

Business Transactions

Record each transaction as a journal entry. Debits must equal credits before an entry can be posted.

General journal

Trial Balance

Ten-column worksheet reduced to six: balances before adjustments, the adjusting entries, and balances after. Each pair of totals should match.

Adjustments

Adjusting entries dated at period end. They update the trial balance but stay separate from day-to-day transactions.
